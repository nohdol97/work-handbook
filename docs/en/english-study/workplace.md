# Interview and workplace expressions

## Expressions in the corpus — by frequency

### X depends on Y {#workplace-depends-on}

**48 occurrences · 25 documents · neutral**

**X는 Y에 따라 달라진다**

Give a conditional answer in an interview, then name the factors that matter. Do not stop at It depends.

> The right batch size depends on sequence length and available GPU memory.
>
> 적절한 batch 크기는 sequence 길이와 사용 가능한 GPU 메모리에 따라 달라진다.

Counted forms: depends on, depend on.

### X does not mean Y {#workplace-does-not-mean}

**47 occurrences · 32 documents · neutral**

**X라고 해서 Y라는 뜻은 아니다**

Correct an assumed equivalence between two facts. Follow mean with a noun phrase or a that-clause. Explain the condition without blaming the listener.

> A successful retry does not mean the original request failed.
>
> 재시도가 성공했다고 해서 원래 요청이 실패했다는 뜻은 아니다.

Counted forms: does not mean, doesn't mean.

Example source pages: [ai-model-development/adapter-compatibility](../ai-model-development/adapter-compatibility.md), [ai-model-development/evaluation-promotion](../ai-model-development/evaluation-promotion.md), [ai-model-development/qlora-artifacts](../ai-model-development/qlora-artifacts.md).

### X does not guarantee Y {#workplace-does-not-guarantee}

**33 occurrences · 24 documents · neutral**

**X가 Y를 보장하지는 않는다**

Explain a design limit or distinguish a necessary condition from a sufficient one. Follow guarantee with an outcome noun or a that-clause.

> Adding replicas does not guarantee lower latency when the database is the bottleneck.
>
> 데이터베이스가 병목이면 replica를 늘려도 latency 감소가 보장되지는 않는다.

Counted forms: does not guarantee, doesn't guarantee.

Example source pages: [ai-model-development/adapter-compatibility](../ai-model-development/adapter-compatibility.md), [ai-model-development/artifact-lineage](../ai-model-development/artifact-lineage.md), [ai-model-development/model-developer](../ai-model-development/model-developer.md).

### Check that ... {#workplace-check-that}

**28 occurrences · 26 documents · neutral**

**...인지 점검하다**

Ask someone to verify an expected condition. Check whether often investigates an open possibility; check that fits a condition that should hold.

> Check that the artifact checksum matches the checksum recorded during evaluation.
>
> Artifact checksum이 평가 때 기록한 checksum과 일치하는지 점검한다.

Counted forms: check that.

Example source pages: [aws-cloud/compute](../aws-cloud/compute.md), [aws-cloud/eks](../aws-cloud/eks.md), [aws-cloud/foundations](../aws-cloud/foundations.md).

### For example, ... {#workplace-for-example}

**18 occurrences · 12 documents · neutral**

**예를 들어 ...**

Add a concrete case after an abstract technical explanation. Do not turn one example into a guarantee for every environment.

> For example, a slow downstream API can increase consumer lag.
>
> 예를 들어 느린 downstream API는 consumer lag을 늘릴 수 있다.

Counted forms: for example.

Example source pages: [ai-model-development/model-developer](../ai-model-development/model-developer.md), [ai-model-development/training-datasets](../ai-model-development/training-datasets.md), [aws-cloud/ai-gpu-architecture](../aws-cloud/ai-gpu-architecture.md).

### Check whether ... {#workplace-check-whether}

**14 occurrences · 12 documents · neutral**

**...인지 확인하다**

Investigate a condition whose truth is still unknown. Follow whether with a clause containing a subject and a verb.

> Check whether the errors started before or after the deployment.
>
> 오류가 배포 전과 후 중 언제 시작됐는지 확인한다.

Counted forms: check whether.

### Compared with X, Y ... {#workplace-compared-with}

**4 occurrences · 3 documents · neutral**

> Compared with the baseline model, this candidate uses less memory but answers more slowly.
>
> Baseline 모델과 비교하면 이 후보는 메모리를 덜 쓰지만 답변이 더 느리다.

Counted forms: compared with.

> 목표는 성공 응답을 보낸 쓰기를 잃지 않으면서 복구 시간을 줄이는 것이다.

### From a platform perspective, ... {#workplace-platform-perspective}

**3 occurrences · 3 documents · neutral**

**플랫폼 관점에서 보면 ...**

> From a platform perspective, every artifact needs a traceable version and a clear owner.
>
> 플랫폼 관점에서 보면 모든 artifact에는 추적 가능한 버전과 명확한 담당자가 필요하다.

### In this case, ... {#workplace-in-this-case}

**3 occurrences · 2 documents · neutral**

**이 경우에는 ...**

Limit a decision to the conditions just described. Make those conditions clear in the preceding sentence.

> The cache is empty. In this case, the request must read from the database.
>
> Cache가 비어 있다. 이 경우 요청은 데이터베이스에서 읽어야 한다.

Counted forms: in this case.

Example source pages: [platform-infrastructure/developer-platform](../platform-infrastructure/developer-platform.md), [platform-infrastructure/kubernetes-operations](../platform-infrastructure/kubernetes-operations.md).

### One possible cause is ... {#workplace-possible-cause}

**1 occurrence · 1 document · neutral**

**가능한 원인 하나는 ...이다**

Present an unconfirmed cause as a hypothesis during incident analysis. Add a next check instead of treating it as a conclusion.

> One possible cause is a mismatch between the adapter and the base model.
>
> 가능한 원인 하나는 adapter와 base model의 불일치다.

Counted forms: one possible cause is, one possible cause.

Example source pages: [ai-model-development/evaluation-promotion](../ai-model-development/evaluation-promotion.md).

### The key point is ... {#workplace-key-point}

**1 occurrence · 1 document · neutral**

**핵심은 ...이다**

Highlight one central condition after a longer explanation. Continue with a noun phrase or a that-clause.

> The key point is that validation data must remain separate from training data.
>
> 핵심은 validation 데이터를 training 데이터와 분리해야 한다는 점이다.

Counted forms: the key point is.

### As far as I can tell, ... {#workplace-as-far-as}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**제가 확인한 범위에서는 ...**

Share a tentative judgment when visibility is limited. Separate what you checked from what remains unknown.

> As far as I can tell, existing replicas are healthy, but new replicas cannot load the model.
>
> 제가 확인한 범위에서는 기존 replica는 정상이지만 새 replica는 모델을 불러오지 못한다.

Counted forms: as far as i can tell.

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**확인한 근거를 바탕으로 보면 ...**

> Based on the evidence, the delay appears to occur during model loading.

Counted forms: based on the evidence.

### Could you clarify ...? {#workplace-clarify}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

> Latency 목표에 네트워크 시간도 포함되는지 명확히 설명해 주시겠어요?

### Does that mean ...? {#workplace-does-that-mean}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

>
> 그렇다면 adapter마다 별도의 base model 복사본이 필요하다는 뜻인가요?

### I do not have enough information to ... {#workplace-insufficient-information}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**...하기에는 정보가 충분하지 않습니다**

Withhold a conclusion when evidence is insufficient. Say what additional information would help move the discussion forward.

> I do not have enough information to recommend a larger GPU; I need the peak memory usage first.
>
> 더 큰 GPU를 권하기에는 정보가 충분하지 않습니다. 먼저 최대 메모리 사용량이 필요합니다.

Counted forms: i do not have enough information to, i don't have enough information to.

### I would start by ... {#workplace-start-by}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**저라면 먼저 ...하겠습니다**

Describe your first step in a hypothetical interview problem. Use an -ing form after by. Would marks a proposed approach, not a claim of past experience.

> I would start by comparing the failure rate before and after the configuration change.
>
> 저라면 먼저 설정 변경 전후의 실패율을 비교하겠습니다.

Counted forms: i would start by.

### Let me confirm ... {#workplace-confirm}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**...을 확인하겠습니다**

Restate an agreement or check your understanding of a requirement. This does not claim that the fact is already verified.

> Let me confirm the requirement: should a failed evaluation block deployment?
>
> 요구사항을 확인하겠습니다. 평가가 실패하면 배포를 차단해야 하나요?

Counted forms: let me confirm.

### Let me walk you through ... {#workplace-walk-through}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · informal**

**...을 순서대로 설명드리겠습니다**

Introduce a step-by-step explanation in an interview or design meeting. This conversational expression suits speech better than a long document title.

> Let me walk you through the request path from the gateway to the model server.

> Gateway에서 model server까지 요청이 흐르는 경로를 순서대로 설명드리겠습니다.

Counted forms: let me walk you through.

### The trade-off is ... {#workplace-tradeoff}

>
> 메모리 사용은 줄지만 학습 중 연산이 늘어나는 점을 감수해야 한다.

Counted forms: the trade-off is.

### We are still investigating ... {#workplace-still-investigating}

>
> Timeout은 계속 조사 중이며 다음으로 downstream 서비스 로그를 확인할 예정입니다.

### We have ruled out ... {#workplace-ruled-out}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**...은 원인 후보에서 제외했습니다**

Use this when checks support excluding a possible cause. It does not mean that you merely failed to confirm it.

> We have ruled out expired credentials by verifying the token validity and access logs.
>
> Token 유효성과 접근 로그를 확인해 자격증명 만료는 원인 후보에서 제외했습니다.

Counted forms: we have ruled out.

### We need to ... {#workplace-we-need-to}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**우리는 ...할 필요가 있다**

State a next action or prerequisite clearly. Give the reason, and do not imply team agreement that has not been reached.

> We need to check the failure logs before changing the timeout.
>
> Timeout을 바꾸기 전에 실패 로그를 확인할 필요가 있다.

Counted forms: we need to.

### What would happen if ...? {#workplace-what-if}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

>
> 같은 이벤트가 두 번 도착한다면 어떻게 되나요?

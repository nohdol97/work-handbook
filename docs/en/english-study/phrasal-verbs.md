## Expressions in the corpus — by frequency

### narrow down {#phrasal-verbs-narrow-down}

**3 occurrences · 2 documents · neutral**

**후보나 범위를 줄이다**

Use this transitive phrasal verb to reduce a set of options or possible causes. It is separable: narrow it down. Use to for the remaining scope or number.

> Can these logs narrow down the possible causes?
>
> 이 로그로 가능한 원인 후보를 줄일 수 있나요?

Counted forms: narrow down, narrows down, narrowed down, narrowing down.

Example source pages: [platform-infrastructure/architecture](../platform-infrastructure/architecture.md), [platform-infrastructure/kubernetes-operations](../platform-infrastructure/kubernetes-operations.md).

Usage references: [1](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/narrow-down).

### roll back {#phrasal-verbs-roll-back}

**2 occurrences · 2 documents · neutral**

**이전 상태나 버전으로 되돌리다**

In technical work, this phrasal verb means restoring an earlier state or version. It can take an object and is separable: roll it back. Distinguish it from the noun rollback.

> Can we roll back this deployment safely?
>
> 이 배포를 안전하게 이전 상태로 되돌릴 수 있나요?

Counted forms: roll back, rolls back, rolled back, rolling back.

Example source pages: [data-platform/production-operations](../data-platform/production-operations.md), [platform-infrastructure/terraform-iac](../platform-infrastructure/terraform-iac.md).

Usage references: [1](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/roll-back).

### rule out {#phrasal-verbs-rule-out}

**2 occurrences · 1 document · neutral**

**가능성에서 제외하다**

Use this transitive phrasal verb to exclude a cause or option. It is separable: rule it out. Do not claim a cause is excluded without evidence.

> Can we rule out a network timeout?
>
> 네트워크 시간 초과 가능성을 배제할 수 있나요?

Counted forms: rule out, rules out, ruled out, ruling out.

Example source pages: [platform-infrastructure/linux-containers](../platform-infrastructure/linux-containers.md).

### scale out {#phrasal-verbs-scale-out}

**2 occurrences · 2 documents · neutral**

**인스턴스나 복제본 수를 늘려 확장하다**

This is a technical expression. When contrasting horizontal and vertical scaling, scale out usually adds instances and scale up adds resources per instance. Some contexts, including Kubernetes, also use scale up for adding replicas, so check what is being scaled.

> When should the service scale out?
>
> 서비스는 언제 인스턴스 수를 늘려야 하나요?

Counted forms: scale out, scales out, scaled out, scaling out.

Example source pages: [aws-cloud/ai-gpu-architecture](../aws-cloud/ai-gpu-architecture.md), [platform-infrastructure/postgresql](../platform-infrastructure/postgresql.md).

Usage references: [1](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/autoscaling/ec2/userguide/ec2-auto-scaling-lifecycle.html), [2](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/autoscaling/horizontal-pod-autoscale/).

### hand over {#phrasal-verbs-hand-over}

**1 occurrence · 1 document · neutral**

**업무·책임·물건을 인계하다**

Use this transitive phrasal verb to transfer work, responsibility, or an item. It is separable: hand it over. Put the recipient after to; take over describes the receiving side.

> When should we hand over the runbook to the support team?
>
> 지원 팀에 운영 절차서를 언제 인계해야 하나요?

Counted forms: hand over, hands over, handed over, handing over.

Example source pages: [ai-model-development/model-platform-collaboration](../ai-model-development/model-platform-collaboration.md).

Usage references: [1](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/hand-over).

### take over {#phrasal-verbs-take-over}

**1 occurrence · 1 document · neutral**

**업무나 책임을 넘겨받다**

Use this phrasal verb for assuming responsibility. It can stand alone or take an object, as in take over the task. With a pronoun, use take it over.

> Who can take over this task tomorrow?
>
> 내일 누가 이 작업을 이어서 맡을 수 있나요?

Counted forms: take over, takes over, took over, taken over, taking over.

### break down {#phrasal-verbs-break-down}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**복잡한 대상을 작은 부분으로 나누다**

The sense here is transitive: divide something into smaller parts. It is separable: break it down. The separate intransitive sense means stop working; counts do not distinguish senses.

> Can you break down the migration into three stages?
>
> 마이그레이션을 세 단계로 나눠 설명해 주시겠어요?

Counted forms: break down, breaks down, broke down, broken down, breaking down.

### figure out {#phrasal-verbs-figure-out}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · informal**

**생각하거나 조사해서 알아내다**

Use this transitive phrasal verb to understand or find an answer. It is separable: figure it out. It is natural in conversation; determine may suit a formal report.

> How can we figure out which request failed?
>
> 어느 요청이 실패했는지 어떻게 알아낼 수 있을까요?

Counted forms: figure out, figures out, figured out, figuring out.

### shut down {#phrasal-verbs-shut-down}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

### turn out {#phrasal-verbs-turn-out}

**0 occurrences · 0 documents · neutral**

**결국 ~로 드러나다, 밝혀지다**

This intransitive phrasal verb introduces a result or discovery. Use it turns out that plus a clause, or turn out to be. It takes no direct object in this sense.

> It may turn out that the limit is too low.
>
> 그 제한값이 너무 낮은 것으로 드러날 수도 있다.

Counted forms: turn out, turns out, turned out, turning out.
